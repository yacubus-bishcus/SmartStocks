Add a function like:
generate_price_interval(ticker, horizon_days=30, confidence=0.90)
Outputs:
lower_bound
median_estimate
upper_bound
probability_up_5_percent
probability_down_5_percent
expected_move
volatility_regime
Use historical bootstrap first. Then add GARCH and jump diffusion.
Phase 2: Swing detector
Add:
calculate_swing_score(ticker, horizon_days=20)
Outputs:
swing_probability
volatility_regime
compression_score
volume_anomaly_score
breakout_risk
Phase 3: Inflection detector
Add:
calculate_inflection_score(ticker, horizon_days=10)
Outputs:
bullish_inflection_probability
bearish_inflection_probability
no_inflection_probability

My recommended model stack
For your first serious version:
Price interval:
Historical bootstrap + EWMA volatility + Monte Carlo jump diffusion

Volatility:
Realized volatility + ATR + GARCH/EGARCH

Swing probability:
Random Forest or XGBoost classifier

Inflection probability:
Logistic regression baseline, then Random Forest/XGBoost

Qualitative:
Structured news/filing/macro scraper + LLM summarizer

Validation:
Walk-forward backtesting
I would not start with LSTM or Transformer models yet. Get the labels, features, confidence intervals, and backtesting right first. Deep learning can come later.
The main shift is this:

Smart Stocks should become a probabilistic market risk and catalyst engine, not a price-prediction engine.
That means your app tells the user:
how wide the possible move is, how likely a large move is, whether the trend may be near a turn, and what real-world catalysts could break the historical pattern.
